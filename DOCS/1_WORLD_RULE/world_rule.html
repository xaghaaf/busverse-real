FOUNDATIONAL DOCUMENT // v1.0

World Rules

The constitution of BusVerse Real. This document defines what the platform is, what it is not, how it behaves under pressure, and what rules never change. Every other document in BusVerse derives from these rules. If any feature, flow, or design contradicts this document, the feature is wrong.

Version: 1.0 Status: Locked Owner: Founder Applies to: All BusVerse Real systems
Preamble

BusVerse Real is a real bus booking platform operating in India. It connects verified bus operators with real passengers, handles real money, and moves people between real cities. Unlike a demo or simulation, every transaction on BusVerse Real has real-world consequences. Because of this, ambiguity is dangerous. Every question must have one answer. Every rule must be explicit. This document exists to remove ambiguity from the system before a single line of product code is written.

Article 01

Identity

What BusVerse Real is. Every downstream decision derives from this.

1.1
BusVerse Real is a marketplace
It connects two sides — bus operators who own buses and passengers who buy seats. BusVerse does not own buses. BusVerse does not employ drivers. BusVerse does not set final fares. BusVerse enables transactions between the two sides.
1.2
BusVerse is operated by a single founder (Phase 1)
No team. No co-founder. No investors. This means every rule must assume one human being handles production incidents, support, and development. Rules that require a team (24/7 rotation, multi-person approvals) are deferred until Phase 2.
1.3
BusVerse operates pan-India, starting with one region
The platform is built for multi-city, multi-state operation from day one. Launch, however, is region-scoped. The first region is chosen for operator density and route demand — likely Delhi-NCR to Rajasthan, or Mumbai–Pune corridor.
1.4
BusVerse supports intercity and intracity travel
Intercity (Delhi → Jaipur) is the primary use case. Intracity (within-city routes) is supported by the same platform but launched later. The data model treats both uniformly.
1.5
BusVerse integrates private and government operators
Private operators onboard directly. Government buses (state transport corporations) are integrated as a separate class — with distinct branding, subsidized fares, and separate settlement rules.
1.6
BusVerse earns through a passenger convenience fee
Operators pay nothing to list. Passengers pay a small convenience fee (₹10–₹20) disclosed at checkout. This is the primary revenue stream for Phase 1. Additional revenue streams may be added later but never at the operator's expense.

Article 03

Actors

Five human actor types interact with BusVerse. Each has a distinct identity, permissions, and life cycle.

3.1
Passenger
A person who searches, books, pays for, and travels on a seat. Identified by phone number. Can book as guest (limited) or registered user (full). A passenger may also be a driver or operator in different contexts, but the roles never mix within a single session.
3.2
Driver
A licensed person who drives a bus for an operator. Onboarded by the operator. Verified by BusVerse via KYC. Uses the Driver App. Can receive trip assignments, stream GPS, scan tickets, and report delays. Never books tickets as a passenger while on duty.
3.3
Operator
A company that owns buses and employs drivers. Identified by GSTIN. Onboards via the Operator Panel, verified by BusVerse admin. Creates routes, schedules, fares. Manages fleet and drivers. Receives settlements. Cannot be a passenger, driver, or admin.
3.4
Admin
The BusVerse operations team (Phase 1: the founder). Approves operators, buses, and routes. Handles disputes, refunds, and fraud. Has full read access to every record with an immutable audit log. Cannot modify bookings directly — only cancel or refund with reason.
3.5
Support
Subset of admin for customer service. Handles tickets, live chat, and low-value refunds. Cannot approve operator KYC, modify routes, or access financial configuration. Escalates to admin when needed.
3.6
Government operator (special class)
State transport corporations. Onboarded by admin, not self-service. Follow different KYC rules, use subsidized fares, and settle via government channels. Appear in search with distinct branding.
Article 04

Money Rules

Real INR. Real gateways. Real consequences. These rules govern every rupee that moves through the platform.

4.1
All payments flow through licensed gateways
BusVerse never holds customer funds directly. Every payment goes through an RBI-licensed Payment Aggregator — Razorpay, PhonePe, Paytm, or Cashfree. BusVerse's role is to initiate payments and receive settlements. It is not a Payment Aggregator itself.
4.2
Fare + convenience fee is the only amount charged
The passenger pays the operator's fare plus the BusVerse convenience fee. No hidden charges. No taxes added after. The total shown at checkout is the exact amount debited. GST is included in the fare, not added on top.
4.3
Money is idempotent
Every payment carries a unique idempotency key. Retrying a payment never charges twice. If the gateway processes the same payment twice (rare but possible), the duplicate is auto-detected and refunded within 24 hours.
4.4
Refunds go to the original payment method
UPI refund goes to UPI. Card refund goes to card. BusVerse never redirects a refund to a different account, even if requested by the passenger. This is a legal requirement and a fraud prevention measure.
4.5
Refunds are never instant
BusVerse initiates refunds within minutes of cancellation. The gateway then takes 3–7 business days to credit the passenger's account. BusVerse never promises instant refunds. The user sees the actual status at every step.
4.6
Operators are settled after the refund window closes
Operators do not receive payment immediately after a booking. Settlement happens after the trip completes and the refund window closes (typically T+7). This protects BusVerse from chargebacks and operator-failure scenarios.
4.7
Gateway fees are absorbed by BusVerse
For UPI, gateway fees are zero (as per NPCI rules). For cards and netbanking, the gateway charges a fee that BusVerse absorbs — it is not passed to the passenger or deducted from the operator's settlement. This is factored into the convenience fee model.
4.8
No wallet for Phase 1
BusVerse does not offer a proprietary wallet in Phase 1. Wallets introduce RBI compliance, KYC overhead, and float management — none of which are worth it until scale demands it. Payments are always direct to gateway.
Article 05

Time Rules

Time is used inconsistently in most systems. BusVerse is explicit about which clock each system uses.

5.1
All user-facing times are in IST
Departure, arrival, boarding, ETA — every time shown to a user is in Indian Standard Time (UTC+5:30). No time-zone conversion for the user. Buses in India operate on IST regardless of where the app is used.
5.2
All internal timestamps are in UTC
Every record in the database stores time in UTC. Conversion to IST happens only at the presentation layer. This ensures correctness across DST transitions (which India does not observe but future expansion might) and simplifies time-zone-aware queries.
5.3
Seat locks are always real-time
A seat lock lasts exactly 5 minutes in real wall-clock time. It does not scale with any simulation factor. There is no simulation mode in BusVerse Real. If a user's clock differs from the server's clock, the server always wins.
5.4
Cancellation windows use real time
"More than 24 hours before departure" is measured in real IST against the scheduled departure time. Not in business hours. Not excluding weekends. Real time only.
5.5
Schedules are always in IST
A bus scheduled for 21:45 departs at 21:45 IST. If the bus is late, the delay is measured against this IST schedule. All ETA calculations output IST.
5.6
Server time is authoritative
The server's clock is the single source of truth. If a client's clock is off by hours, the client is wrong. The server rejects any request that relies on client time for critical decisions (payment confirmation, seat lock expiry, cancellation timing).
Article 06

Data Rules

Personal data, business data, and system data. Who owns what, who sees what, how long it lives.

6.1
Users own their personal data
Name, phone, email, and ID details belong to the user. BusVerse holds them only to enable bookings and support. Users can export or delete their data on request, subject to legal retention requirements.
6.2
Operators own their booking data
Operators can access every booking made on their buses — passenger names, seats, boarding points, fare collected. Operators cannot see bookings on other operators' buses. BusVerse does not sell or share operator data.
6.3
Passwords and PINs are never stored in plain text
Passwords are hashed with bcrypt or Argon2. UPI PINs are never stored by BusVerse at all — they are entered directly on the gateway's hosted page. Card numbers are tokenized by the gateway; BusVerse sees only the last 4 digits.
6.4
PII is masked in logs
Phone numbers, email addresses, and ID numbers are masked before they reach any log file. 9876543210 becomes 9876XXXXX0 in logs. No raw PII appears in Sentry, CloudWatch, or any monitoring tool.
6.5
Booking data is retained for 8 years
GST and income tax laws require booking and payment records for 8 years. Refund records similarly. After 8 years, records are archived or purged in compliance with legal requirements.
6.6
GPS data is retained for 90 days
Position history from driver phones is retained for 90 days for dispute resolution, safety investigations, and ETA accuracy analysis. After 90 days, only aggregated data is kept. Raw GPS traces are deleted.
6.7
Audit logs are never deleted
Every admin action, every refund, every dispute resolution, every operator suspension is logged permanently. Audit logs are append-only. No one, not even the founder, can delete an audit entry.
6.8
Data is stored in India
All user data, booking data, and payment data is stored on servers physically located in India. This is a DPDP Act compliance requirement and will remain true even as the platform scales.
Article 07

Booking Rules

The atomic unit of BusVerse. Every rule here protects the integrity of a booking.

7.1
One seat = one passenger
A seat can never be booked for more than one passenger at a time. If a group travels together, each passenger has their own seat and their own entry on the ticket.
7.2
Seat locks are atomic
The moment a seat is locked, no other user can lock it. This is enforced by the database, not the application. Two concurrent lock attempts on the same seat result in exactly one success and one failure — never two successes, never two failures.
7.3
A lock converts to a booking or expires — never both
If payment succeeds, the lock is deleted and a booking is created in a single atomic transaction. If payment fails or the lock expires, the lock is deleted and no booking exists. There is no intermediate state.
7.4
Payments are never partial
A booking is either fully paid or not created. There is no "half-paid" state. If a passenger tries to pay ₹500 for a ₹1000 fare, the payment fails and the booking is not created. Partial payments are not a feature.
7.5
Confirmed bookings cannot be silently modified
Once a booking is confirmed, the seat number, passenger name, and fare cannot be changed without the passenger's explicit action. Operators cannot move passengers to different seats without notifying them and getting confirmation (or issuing a refund).
7.6
Every booking has exactly one ticket
A booking generates exactly one ticket with one QR code. The ticket is immutable once issued. If the booking is cancelled, the ticket becomes invalid but is not deleted — it is marked cancelled for audit purposes.
7.7
Guest bookings have limited features
A guest can search, book, and pay. A guest cannot access booking history, cannot use support chat, and cannot receive refunds to a different contact. To unlock full features, they must register.
7.8
Every booking is auditable forever
Every state change of a booking (created, paid, confirmed, checked-in, cancelled, refunded, completed) is recorded with a timestamp, actor, and reason. The complete lifecycle of any booking can be reconstructed years later.
Article 08

Cancellation & Refund Rules

The refund policy is public. Every rupee is accounted for. No exceptions.

6–12 hours before departure75% of fare + 100% of feePassenger
2–6 hours before departure50% of fare + 100% of feePassenger

8.1
The convenience fee is always refunded on cancellation
Regardless of when the passenger cancels, the BusVerse convenience fee is fully refunded. The refund policy only applies to the operator's fare. This ensures passengers are never penalized for using BusVerse.
8.2
Cancellations by the operator always refund 100%
If the operator cancels a trip, every passenger receives a full refund regardless of timing. Additionally, the operator is penalized in their quality score. Repeated cancellations can lead to suspension.
8.3
Refunds are initiated within 15 minutes
When a cancellation is confirmed, the refund is initiated to the gateway within 15 minutes. The passenger sees "Refund initiated" immediately. The gateway then takes 3–7 business days to credit the account.
8.4
No manual refunds for standard cancellations
Every standard cancellation is refunded automatically by the refund engine. Manual refunds are only issued for exceptional cases (disputes, fraud, gateways failures) and require admin approval with a reason.
8.5
Refunds cannot be redirected
Even if a passenger asks, a refund goes to the original payment method. No exceptions. This is a hard rule that protects BusVerse from fraud and complies with RBI and PCI-DSS requirements.
Article 09

Passenger Rules

What a passenger can do, cannot do, and what BusVerse owes them.

9.1
Passenger can cancel any booking before departure
No booking is locked-in. A passenger can cancel any confirmed booking up to 2 minutes before departure, receiving the refund due per the policy. After departure, no cancellation is possible.
9.2
Passenger sees exactly where their bus is
Once the driver starts the trip, the passenger sees the bus moving on the map in real time. If GPS data is temporarily unavailable, the passenger sees the last known position with a "stale" indicator — never a fake position.
9.3
Passenger cannot contact the driver directly
For safety and liability reasons, passengers cannot call, message, or contact the driver. All communication flows through BusVerse support. Emergency SOS alerts the platform, not the driver.
9.4
Passenger can share their live trip
A passenger can generate a shareable live-tracking link (via WhatsApp or SMS) that shows their trip in real time. This link works without login for anyone who has it. It can be revoked at any time.
9.5
Passenger can rate every trip
After each completed trip, the passenger can rate the operator and driver on a 1–5 scale. Ratings are public on the operator profile. Fake ratings are filtered out by a fraud detection system.
9.6
Passenger cannot be discriminated against
BusVerse does not allow operators to refuse boarding based on caste, religion, gender, or disability. Any such incident reported and verified leads to immediate operator suspension.
9.7
Passenger safety is non-negotiable
If a passenger feels unsafe, SOS triggers an immediate alert to BusVerse support with live location. Support responds within 2 minutes during daytime, 5 minutes at night. In critical cases, local authorities are contacted.
Article 10

Driver Rules

A driver represents the operator and BusVerse. Behavior on the road defines the platform's reputation.

10.1
Driver is verified before first trip
A driver must have a valid commercial license, KYC verification, and operator authorization before any trip assignment. If any document expires, the driver is blocked from new trips until renewed.
10.2
Driver streams GPS during active trips
The Driver App must stream location every 10 seconds while a trip is active. If GPS is disabled, the app prompts the driver to enable it. If the driver refuses, the trip is flagged to admin and passengers see "GPS unavailable."
10.3
Driver scans every boarding passenger
Every passenger's QR is scanned before boarding. Unscanned passengers cannot board. This ensures accurate manifests, prevents fraud, and helps with safety investigations.
10.4
Driver reports delays immediately
If a trip is delayed by more than 15 minutes, the driver must pick a reason (traffic, breakdown, rest stop, medical) and report it. This triggers notifications to passengers with an updated ETA.
10.5
Driver cannot cancel a trip
Only the operator can cancel a trip. A driver who cannot operate a bus must contact the operator, who then decides to cancel or reassign. Passengers are protected from last-minute cancellations without consequence.
10.6
Driver has emergency authority
In an emergency (medical, security, breakdown), the driver has full authority to deviate from the route, stop the bus, or reroute to the nearest safe point. BusVerse support is notified automatically. The driver's decision is protected from penalty in genuine emergencies.
Article 11

Operator Rules

Operators own the buses and the responsibility. BusVerse holds them to a standard.

11.1
Operator is verified before listing any bus
GST, PAN, company registration, and bank account are verified before any bus goes live. Buses need valid registration, insurance, and fitness certificates. No exceptions.
11.2
Operator is responsible for the trip
If the bus breaks down, if the driver doesn't show up, if the trip is cancelled — the operator is responsible. BusVerse facilitates the booking and handles the refund, but the operational failure is the operator's liability.
11.3
Operator cannot see other operators' data
An operator sees only their own buses, routes, bookings, drivers, and revenue. Cross-operator data is never visible. BusVerse does not share competitor intelligence.
11.4
Operator pays no commission in Phase 1
BusVerse does not charge commission. The operator receives the full fare. The passenger pays the convenience fee. This is a deliberate Phase 1 strategy to attract operators without competing with RedBus on commission.
11.5
Operator is settled after the refund window
Settlements happen after the trip completes and the refund window closes. Typically T+7. This protects BusVerse from chargebacks if a passenger disputes a fare after travel.
11.6
Operator quality is scored continuously
Every operator has a quality score based on on-time performance, cancellation rate, complaint count, and passenger ratings. The score affects search ranking. Operators with consistently low scores are suspended.
11.7
Operator cannot discriminate
Operators cannot refuse boarding based on caste, religion, gender, or disability. Violations lead to immediate suspension and potential legal action. BusVerse stands with passengers in every verified discrimination case.
Article 12

Admin & Support Rules

Admin has power. Support has limited power. Both have accountability.

12.1
Every admin action is logged
Approving an operator, rejecting a bus, issuing a refund, suspending a user, modifying a route — every action is logged with timestamp, admin ID, reason, and affected record. Logs are append-only.
12.2
Admin cannot modify a confirmed booking
Admin can cancel a booking with a reason, but cannot change the seat, passenger, or fare of a confirmed booking. If a change is needed, the booking is cancelled and a new one is created — full audit trail preserved.
12.3
Admin cannot access passwords or PINs
Passwords are hashed. PINs are never stored. Admin cannot reset passwords — users reset their own via OTP. Admin cannot see card details — only last 4 digits and token reference.
12.4
Support refunds are capped
Support agents can issue refunds up to ₹5,000 per booking without approval. Refunds above ₹5,000 require admin approval. This prevents abuse while allowing support to resolve common cases quickly.
12.5
Admin cannot bypass legal compliance
Admin cannot disable GST invoicing. Admin cannot delete audit logs. Admin cannot modify booking records retroactively. Admin cannot process refunds to non-original payment methods. These constraints are enforced by code, not by policy.
12.6
Every dispute is arbitrated with reason
When a passenger and operator disagree, admin reviews evidence and issues a decision with a written reason. The decision is logged and communicated to both sides. There is no silent resolution.
Article 13

Failure Rules

What happens when things break. Because they will.

13.1
Money never silently disappears
If a payment is debited but the booking fails, the payment is detected, logged, and refunded automatically within 24 hours. If a refund is initiated but the gateway fails, the refund is retried and monitored. A passenger never loses money without a record.
13.2
Seat locks fail safe
If Redis (the lock store) crashes, the system falls back to PostgreSQL for locks. If PostgreSQL also fails, the system refuses new bookings rather than risk double-booking. A failed lock is always better than a wrong lock.
13.3
GPS failures degrade gracefully
If GPS data stops for a trip, passengers see "GPS unavailable — last known position at HH:MM." The ETA switches to schedule-based estimate. The passenger is never shown a fake or frozen position.
13.4
Payment gateway failure does not block bookings
If one gateway is down, the payment adapter automatically routes to an alternate gateway. If all gateways are down, bookings are disabled with a clear message — "Payments temporarily unavailable, please try again in a few minutes."
13.5
WebSocket failure does not break the app
If WebSocket connections drop, the client falls back to HTTP polling every 3 seconds. The user experience is slightly degraded (slower updates) but functional. The client attempts WebSocket reconnection continuously.
13.6
Trip cancellation is always refunded
If a trip is cancelled for any reason — operator, weather, breakdown, government order — every passenger is fully refunded. There is no scenario where a passenger pays for a trip that does not happen and does not receive a refund.
13.7
Every failure is logged and reviewed
Every failure — payment, lock, GPS, gateway, refund — is logged with enough context to reproduce and fix. Failures are reviewed daily. Patterns are addressed with code changes, not workarounds.
Article 14

Immutable Rules

Rules that never change, regardless of pressure, growth, or opportunity. These are the constitution within the constitution.

These rules cannot be overridden by code, config, admin action, or business pressure. If any of these is ever violated, it is a critical bug that must be fixed within 24 hours.

14.1
Never charge a passenger twice for the same seat
Idempotency is enforced at the database level. No scenario — retry, network glitch, race condition — can result in a double charge.
14.2
Never redirect a refund
Refunds always go to the original payment method. No exceptions, no admin override, no passenger request.
14.3
Never sell or share personal data
No user data is ever sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising without explicit consent. Data is used only to operate BusVerse.
14.4
Never store raw card data or PINs
Card numbers and UPI PINs never touch BusVerse servers. All card data is handled by the PCI-DSS compliant gateway.
14.5
Never show a fake bus position
If GPS data is unavailable, the app shows the last known position with a stale indicator. It never extrapolates, guesses, or fakes a position.
14.6
Never allow a seat to be double-booked
Atomic locking is enforced at the database level. There is no scenario where two passengers hold the same seat on the same trip.
14.7
Never delete an audit log
Audit logs are append-only. No code, admin, or database operation can remove an audit entry. If an audit entry is wrong, a corrective entry is added.
14.8
Never compromise passenger safety
If a safety feature must be temporarily disabled, the platform enters degraded mode and notifies users. Safety is never traded for revenue, uptime, or speed.
Article 15

Amendment Process

How this document changes. Because it will.

15.1
Rules 1.1 through 15 can be amended
Any rule in this document can be changed, added, or removed — except the immutable rules in Article 14. Amendments require a version bump and a written reason.
15.2
Amendments are logged
Every change to this document is recorded with the date, the rule changed, the reason, and the new version number. The log is kept in the same repository as the document itself, tracked with Git.
15.3
Immutable rules can only be strengthened
Rules in Article 14 can be added to or made stricter. They can never be weakened. If a rule is found to be insufficient, a stronger rule replaces it.
15.4
Every feature must comply with this document
Before building any feature, ask: "Does this comply with the World Rules?" If the answer is no, either the feature is wrong or the rule needs amending first. Never build a feature that contradicts this document.
