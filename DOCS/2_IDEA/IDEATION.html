BusVerse

A real bus booking platform connecting verified operators with passengers across India — intercity and intracity, with real payments, live GPS tracking, and QR-based ticketing.

Version: 1.0 Type: Real Platform · Production Status: Concept Ideation Next: Feature Specification

1. What is BusVerse?

BusVerse is a real bus booking platform that connects verified bus operators with passengers across India. It handles the full journey — from search and seat selection to payment, ticketing, live tracking, and post-trip feedback.

  • Real operators. Real buses. Real passengers.
  • Real money. Real payments. Real refunds.
  • Intercity and intracity travel.
  • Pan-India operation from day one.
  • Government buses integrated where available.
  • Driver app for real drivers with QR scanning and GPS.
  • Operator panel for fleet and route management.
  • Admin oversight with CCTV and dispute resolution.

BusVerse is not a demo. It is a production platform where real passengers book real seats on real buses operated by verified partners.

2. Why BusVerse Exists

2.1 The Problem

Bus travel in India is fragmented. Passengers deal with multiple operators, no unified booking, no live tracking, unclear refunds, and no reliable way to verify the quality of service before booking. Small operators have no digital presence. Government buses are hard to book online.

2.2 The Purpose

  • For passengers: A single platform to search, compare, book, and track buses across all operators.
  • For operators: A digital storefront, a booking engine, and a settlement system — without building it themselves.
  • For drivers: A simple app for trips, tickets, and GPS.
  • For the ecosystem: Trust, safety, and transparency in bus travel.

2.3 The Hook

"Every bus in India — private or government — bookable from one platform, with live tracking, verified operators, and instant refunds."

5. The Experience

5.1 As a Passenger

  1. Open BusVerse. See popular routes and recent searches.
  2. Search for a route: Delhi → Jaipur, pick a date.
  3. Compare buses by price, time, amenities, and operator rating.
  4. Pick a bus. See the live seat map.
  5. Select a seat. It locks for 5 minutes while you pay.
  6. Pay via UPI, card, netbanking, or wallet.
  7. Receive a ticket with a signed QR code (SMS + email + in-app).
  8. Watch the bus move on the map on the day of travel.
  9. Share the trip link with family.
  10. Show the QR to the driver at boarding. Scanned. Checked in.
  11. Trip ends. Rate the operator and driver. Done.

5.2 As a Driver

  1. Log in with phone OTP.
  2. See today's assigned trips.
  3. Start the trip — GPS begins streaming to passengers.
  4. Scan passenger QR tickets at each boarding point.
  5. See the live passenger manifest per stand.
  6. Report delays with a reason.
  7. Trigger SOS if needed.
  8. End the trip. See trip summary.

5.3 As an Operator

  1. Sign up with company details and GST.
  2. Upload KYC — admin verifies.
  3. Add buses with registration, insurance, and seat layout.
  4. Create routes by drawing on a real map.
  5. Create recurring schedules with fares.
  6. Assign drivers to trips.
  7. Monitor bookings, revenue, and cancellations.
  8. View CCTV feeds from buses (where installed).
  9. Receive settlements after each cycle.

5.4 As an Admin

  1. Approve operator KYC and bus registration.
  2. Review and approve routes and schedules.
  3. Monitor all buses on a live map.
  4. Access CCTV feeds across all operators.
  5. Handle disputes, refunds, and chargebacks.
  6. Manage feature flags, content, and announcements.
  7. Audit every action taken on the platform.

7. Booking & Seat Locking

7.1 Booking Flow

Search → select bus → seat map → passenger details → payment → ticket. Every step is atomic and consistent. Two users can never book the same seat.

7.2 Seat Locking

The moment a passenger selects a seat, it locks for 5 minutes. The lock uses Redis SET NX with TTL, falling back to PostgreSQL unique constraints. No two users can lock the same seat.

  • Lock duration: 5 minutes (real time)
  • Extension: once, +2 minutes
  • Max active locks per user: 3
  • Conflict → immediate rejection with error
  • Payment success → lock converts to booking atomically
  • Payment failure/timeout → lock released
  • Page close → lock released (best-effort)
  • Locks never scale with time (real time only)

8. Payments & Refunds

8.1 Payment Gateways

BusVerse supports multiple Indian payment gateways, all through a single Payment Adapter:

  • Razorpay
  • PhonePe
  • Paytm
  • Google Pay (via UPI)
  • Cashfree

8.2 Payment Methods

  • UPI
  • Credit / Debit Cards
  • Netbanking
  • Wallets
  • EMI (selected banks)

8.3 Payment Adapter

One interface, three modes:

  • Simulated: In-app fake UPI screen (for testing without a gateway account)
  • Sandbox: Real gateway in test mode (dummy money)
  • Live: Real money, real transactions

Config-driven. No code changes to switch modes.

8.4 Refund Policy

Time Before DepartureRefund
More than 24 hours100% (minus gateway fee)
12–24 hours90%
6–12 hours75%
2–6 hours50%
Less than 2 hours0%
Operator cancels trip100%
Platform cancels trip100% + compensation credit

8.5 Refund Engine

Event-driven. Listens for cancellation events, checks policy, calls gateway refund API, tracks status via webhooks. Refunds go back to the original payment method (UPI → UPI, card → card). Full audit trail maintained.

9. Ticketing & QR

9.1 Ticket Generation

  • Generated on payment success
  • Unique booking ID
  • Signed QR code (cryptographic signature prevents forgery)
  • Contains route, bus, seat, time, fare, passenger details
  • Delivered via SMS, email, and in-app
  • Downloadable as PDF
  • Viewable offline (PWA cache)

9.2 QR Scanning

  • Driver opens scanner in the Driver App
  • Camera reads the QR
  • Server verifies: signature, booking existence, schedule match, expiry, not already scanned
  • Result: ✅ Valid, ⚠️ Duplicate, ❌ Invalid / expired / cancelled
  • Every scan logged with timestamp and result

9.3 Ticket Lifecycle

Issued → Active → Scanned → Used → Expired. Cancelled at any point before scan. Expired 2 hours after trip ends.

10. Live Tracking

10.1 How It Works

14. Revenue Model

14.1 Primary — Convenience Fee

Passengers pay a small convenience fee per booking (₹10–20). Operators pay nothing. This fee is disclosed upfront at checkout and is BusVerse's primary revenue.

14.2 Additional Revenue Streams

  • Promoted listings (operators pay for higher search placement)
  • Subscription plans for frequent travelers (BusVerse Pass)
  • Corporate B2B booking (service fee per booking)
  • Travel agent commissions
  • Charter / full bus hire commission
  • CCTV hardware/software (if bundled)
  • Partnerships (insurance, last-mile, food)

14.3 Settlement

  • Operator settlement every 7 days (T+7)
  • Settlement after trip completion + refund window closes
  • Detailed statement per trip
  • GST reports for operators
  • Gateway reconciliation daily
